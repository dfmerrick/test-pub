# Citations
All work in this course **must** be cited in accordance with ICD-206. **There are almost no exceptions.**  

ICD 206 is the methodology utilized by the intelligence community to ensure readers of intelligence products understand where data and information is coming from. Within the intelligence community, citations are as critical (or more so) as they are in academia. This course reflects that. **If you do not cite your work, you will likely receive a zero as a grade for that assignment.**

We will discuss and demonstrate ICD 206 in Module 2 of the course.
