
# Homework (HW) 1 - Briefing 1

For this assignment, the student analyst will complete a 60-second video briefing on an assigned topic. Citations are not required for this homework assignment.

#### Instructions:

Student analysts will take the topic assigned (all students will get the same topic) and develop a one-minute video briefing providing background and general information aimed at decision makers. The purpose of the briefing is to provide decision-makers with background information that can assist them in developing courses of action. No in-depth research or analysis is required. This homework is to improve student comfort in providing concise and accurate information in a face-to-face or verbal context.

**Topic: Provide administrators with an overview of FSU Main Campus parking.** 

As a reminder, there should not be any first-person language in this assignment.
#### Requirements:

- The briefing is 60 seconds long.
- The briefing is provided as an online video and is accessible to instructors.
- The briefing includes all types of parking on campus, including (but not limited to) students, faculty and staff, and guests.
- The briefing is structured to provide an overview to decision-makers as they look to change campus parking.
- The briefing is practiced and professional. 

#### Feedback:

Students will receive feedback upon completion of grading. The primary method of feedback will be the grading rubric and instructor-provided comments, with emphasis on areas of deficiency. Student analysts should contact the instructor if the grade assigned is unclear.
