# Semester Intelligence Project (SIP) 2 - Collection and Qualitative Analysis

### Assignment Summary

This step requires the analyst to collect Publicly Available Information (PAI), Commercially Available Information (CAI), and Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) regarding the assigned topic and begin an analysis on the resulting dataset. Student analysts will use the template provided to collect the required information and structure the initial analysis.  

The primary and recommended platform for this assignment is [X.com](http://x.com) (formerly Twitter). Student analysts are expected to create and utilize a free account to perform these actions.  

### Instructions
Student analysts will use the social media application X to collect a sample of 75 posts on the assigned topic. Student analysts will use Search Strings and Operators to find and collect this sample data. Analysts will manually select applicable posts from search results and copy/paste the content of the post into the right column of the template document.  It is recommended that the analyst collect all 75 posts first, before proceeding.

After the sample is collected and pasted into the right column, the analyst will then use highlighters (the highlighter tool in Word, for example) to code the data by theme. Using words or phrases in the post text, the analyst will identify and highlight segments of the post that indicate meaning or applicability to a theme, idea, or context. In the left column for that post, the analyst should enter a brief memo: a rationale or other reason that the post is being coded to a theme. When coding the post, the entirety of the post text should not be highlighted in one color. More than one theme can be highlighted/included in a post. 

The posts that are collected should be a broad representation of the assigned topic. Using the bias discussion in SIP 1, remember that the collection, codes, and memos should all be free of implicit or explicit bias. It is acceptable to note the bias that may be present in a user’s post, but that should be based strictly on the content that is available in the post. For example, it is unacceptable to infer someone's political bias from their post or to assert that they may be left or right-leaning based on one statement. However, if the post says, “I am a republican OR democrat, and I think…” then it is fair to note their political leanings. If there is no explicit expression of their affiliations, the analyst cannot assume. This assignment does not require the analyst to dig into their profile to build a case. This assignment serves to capture information shared in singular tweets to get a brief snapshot of information that is associated with the assigned topic.

Memos can be any information that the analyst thinks is relevant to note about the post. Some of the collected posts may not contribute anything significant, but the product shouldn’t contain more than five (5) posts like that. All posts must include a memo.

The student analyst should use the template document provided in these instructions. Posts and memos should be in 12pt, Times New Roman or Calibri font. There should be between 3 and 5 different colors to code the information; these codes will be used later as themes in the written analysis. The themes identified must be original and cannot come from any examples provided in the course.

The codes must be defined by color in the product. The analyst must be clear about what each color means. For example, “Blue = In support of international intervention.”  These codes can be defined above or below the collected post table.

The last page of the submission is the Search Strings and Operators utilized to collect these posts. Analysts should utilize a wide range of Search Strings and Operators; using one or two search strings will not give you a broad picture and is not acceptable.

### Requirements

- The submission should include 75 posts from [X.com](http://x.com).
- The submission should utilize the document template provided. 
- Fonts should be 12pt Times New Roman or Calibri.
- Search Strings and Operators are required. These replace citations for this assignment.
- There are sufficient Search Strings and Operators used for the assigned topic.
- The submission will include a minimum of three (3) and a maximum of five (5) coded themes.

Standards for this assignment are the same for undergraduate and graduate students.

### No-Go Criteria

Submissions will be assessed first on the following, before any other rubric criteria.  Any failures to meet the foundational requirements will result in an automatic grade of ‘zero’. Further grading of the assignment will not occur.  

- The submission includes properly formatted Search Strings and Operators.  
- The submission includes 75 posts from [X.com](http://x.com).
- The submission uses the supplied template and is formatted correctly, including font.
- The submission includes defined color codes.

Again, if the submission does not pass the ‘no-go’ criteria, it will receive an automatic grade of ‘zero’.  

### Feedback

Students will receive feedback upon completion of grading, focused on the sources/posts selected and the application of codes to these posts.
