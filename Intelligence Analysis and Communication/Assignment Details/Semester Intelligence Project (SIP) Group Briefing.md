# Semester Intelligence Project Group Briefing

### Assignment Summary
Student analysts will present a group briefing to the Class during Week 15.  This is the only group portion of the SIP.  

### Instructions
Analyst Teams (consisting of Student Analysts sharing the same topic) will will create a group briefing. This is the only portion of the SIP that is collaborative. Each student analyst will be graded individually on their contribution and presentation, along with how cohesive the presentation is as a whole. 

Analyst Team members should summarize the material found by all team members during the development of individual SIPs, with emphasis on key points and takeaways. Each Analyst Team has significant latitude in how the presentation fuses each student analyst’s work. The briefing should be accompanied by appropriate visual aids (PowerPoint, etc.)  Presentation materials will not be submitted separately for grading but will be judged by the instructor during the brief.

### Requirements

- The brief should be 9 minutes long. 540 seconds. This is a very specific number.
- Analyst Team members briefing should dress appropriately - business/professional. 
- The Analyst Team should assume they are briefing senior Federal policy makers.
- Citations are NOT required.
### Feedback
Students will receive feedback and questions live in the classroom.
