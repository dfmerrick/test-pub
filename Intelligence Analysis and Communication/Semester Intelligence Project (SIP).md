# Semester Intelligence Project (SIP)
The Semester Intelligence Project (SIP) consists of four (4) deliverables.  Each deliverable will build on previous analyst work, culminating in a group briefing at the end of the semester.  The SIP is designed to lead the student analyst through the entirety of the intelligence cycle, illustrating key concepts and methodology along the way.  

This project will require significant effort on the part of student analysts - plan for that. 

There are hard requirements throughout the SIP, with harsh but realistic penalties associated with these. In the real world, an unsatisfactory product will not receive a poor grade; it will simply be discarded. Analysts are cautioned to understand the ‘no-go’ criteria for each portion of the SIP.  

Throughout this project, students will use ICD 203/ISD 203-1 citations. This is the same format used and required by the U.S. Intelligence Community, and will prepare students for work in the field (or similar areas). This format is not used in academia and will be discussed in a course lecture. 

Topics will be assigned to student analysts in week 2 of the semester. Student analysts will be randomly divided into Analyst Teams. The Analyst Teams will each have a SIP topic assigned, and all student analysts in that team will work on the same broad topic. 

**The SIP is an individual effort - each analyst will produce independent work.** 

The Final Briefing (delivered in class) is a combined effort of the Analyst Team. Each student analyst will contribute to the Final Briefing and participate in the classroom presentation.
