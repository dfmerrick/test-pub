# Intelligence Requests - Spring 2026

Each Semester Intelligence Project (SIP) Group is correlated to an Intel Request below. You will all work individually throughout the semester, and then collaborate on a class briefing (presentation) at the end of the term.    

Many of these request topics are very broad. You may go where your research takes you, while I am not going to specify a course of inquiry - there are some suggestions in the requests. Those suggestions are not all-encompassing but may help you start to brainstorm. Throughout the semester you will find the Semester Intelligence Project assignments start to focus your work. 

For each of these assignments, assume you work for a contractor associated with an Intelligence Community member agency. Your task is deep background and analysis of OSINT and GEOINT - open source, publicly available information. You should include public perception and/or opinion about the topic - something that policy makers may take into account.

Your work does not require access to any material with any type of security classification. The description of the intel request for your group should help you focus your scope. 
## Group 1 - Venezuela (Garnet)

The United States is looking at potential courses of action for stabilizing the Venezuelan government. Your project should focus on mid-2025 and moving forward and should take into account the change of regime in Caracas. You should certainly investigate and discuss oil exports, narcotics trafficking, and linkages to other superpowers.

## Group 2 - Yemen (Garnet)

Yemen and its internal conflict continue to be a concern in the Red Sea region. The United States is looking for potential courses of action to ensure regional stability and freedom of trade in the area. Your product should include a discussion of US and allied military activity, as well as an explanation for the rise and persistence of the Houthis.

## Group 3 - The Com (Garnet)

Cybersecurity and cybercrime are a critical threat to the United States. Your product should include information on The Com as well as similar or allied groups and be directed at decision makers who have little to no knowledge about the topic.

## Group 4 - M23 in Rwanda (Garnet)

Unrest and violence in Rwanda have been a destabilizing factor in the region. The United States is looking to stabilize the area. Decision makers need a detailed report on the background and activities of M23 as well as potential future actions. Your product should include a discussion of the support received by M23, as well as their goals and objectives.

## Group 5 - Sudan (Garnet)

Sudan has been a concern on the African continent for decades. The United States is looking to increase its projection of power to bring stability to the region. Your report is aimed at decision-makers. You have little to no deep information about current conditions, historical trends, or future activities.

## Group 6 - Syria (Garnet)

Despite the fall of the Assad regime, Syria remains a hotspot of violence. U.S. forces have been engaged in the region for a significant amount of time, and the United States is looking for potential courses of action that will result in increasing stability in Syria and the surrounding area. Your report should discuss Russian influence as well as regional powers such as Israel, and what their intent may be.

## Group 7 - Durand Line (Gold)

The border between Afghanistan and Pakistan, known as the Durand Line, is a hotly contested political line on a map. Your report is aimed at decision-makers who have no in-depth knowledge of the traditional cultural and tribal clashes created by this international border. The United States is looking for potential courses of action to stabilize the area, and decision-makers need in-depth background information.
